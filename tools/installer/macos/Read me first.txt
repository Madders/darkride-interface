Darkride for Mac
================

INSTALL
Drag Darkride onto the Applications folder in this window. Then eject the Darkride disk
(click the eject button next to it in a Finder window's sidebar).

OPEN IT THE FIRST TIME
Darkride isn't registered with Apple yet, so the first time, macOS stops it. This happens once:

  1. Open your Applications folder and double-click Darkride. When macOS says it can't open
     it, click Done (or OK). Don't choose Move to Trash.
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, and scroll down to Security. Next to the
     message about Darkride, click "Open Anyway", and enter your Mac's password if asked.
  3. In the window that appears, click "Open Anyway" (or Open).

On macOS 14 Sonoma there's a shortcut: right-click (or Control-click) Darkride in
Applications, choose Open, then click Open.

MICROPHONE
When Darkride asks to use the microphone, click Allow so people near you can hear you.
To change it later: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone.

IF MACOS SAYS DARKRIDE "IS DAMAGED AND CAN'T BE OPENED"
Nothing is broken: it's the same protection. Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities),
paste this line, press Return, then open Darkride again:

  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Darkride.app

WHAT YOU NEED
A Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or later) and macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
