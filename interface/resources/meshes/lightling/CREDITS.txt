The Lightling, Darkride's default avatar

Made by Darkride (2026) from these CC0 models by the VRoid Project (pixiv Inc.), as published on OpenGameArt.org
("VRoid Studio CC0 models"):

- Base_Female.vrm and Base_Male.vrm: the body, face, expressions and skeleton. The Lightling is a shape halfway between
  the two, with the clothes and the original textures removed.
- HairSample_Male.vrm: the short hairstyle (its upright strand removed).
- HairSample_Female.vrm: the twin-tails hairstyle.
