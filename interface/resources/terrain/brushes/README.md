# Brush shapes

The pictures the Terrain tool's Shape chooser offers (terrain::BrushMask, libraries/terrain/src/terrain/BrushMask.h):
greyscale, laid over the brush's square seen from above, white where the brush has its full effect and black where
none. `brushes.json` lists them for the chooser, in its order: name and file. `round.png` is not in the list: it is
the thumbnail of the tool's own round brush, which keeps the Hardness falloff. All were drawn for this folder
(generated shapes, no attribution needed): soft and airbrush gradients, a square, a star, a splotch, scattered dots
and a ring. Load Picture… in the chooser takes any picture from disk for the session.
